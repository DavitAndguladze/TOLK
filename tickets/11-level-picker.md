# 11: Level Picker

**Role:** R2 · **Priority:** P0

**What to build:** A slider shows the upcoming paragraph at each Level, with the Original Level marked. The Reader picks the one they understand; their pick sets the Level and pauses the automatic rule for 2 Pages (D19). Used at onboarding and from a button mid-story.

**Blocked by:** 05 Content in the database, demo Reader signs in, 09 Level rule and the level-change moment

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Dragging re-renders the same paragraph at each Level instantly
- [ ] Picking a Level updates the profile and the next Page
- [ ] Gap between pick and measured performance shows the gentle nudge line
