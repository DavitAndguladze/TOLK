# Progress view — Level Line, Words Learned, Word Bank (ADR-0009)

Supersedes the three-Track design (ADR-0007). Source: raw/reading-app-flow-2026-10-17.md.

- **Level Line:** Level per Chapter (L1 → L5), dot colored by Grading Result. The climb toward the author's original is the demo moment.
- **Words Learned:** running total of Graduated Words, plus the words that graduated this Chapter.
- **Word Bank:** words still being reinforced, with source (missed or flagged) and times seen.
- **Streak** (P1) beside them.
- Each Grading Result shows what it was based on (read-aloud accuracy, Chapter Check score, Flagged Words) and that OCI Generative AI graded it.
