# Reading App: Project Scope & Aim (team doc, Oct 17 2026, @Colin) — ingested verbatim summary

Source: Reading_App_Project_Scope__Aim.docx (uploaded 2026-10-17 17:27)

- **Aim:** assess a user's reading level, then guide practical reading practice across three skills — fluency, vocabulary, comprehension. Progress in each skill visible at all times; every chapter ends with a comprehension check. Long-term: reading as an enjoyable habit, never homework.
- **Problem:** people (kids especially) don't read because it takes more effort than video; nothing pulls them to a book except assignments; many assume reading means sitting 30 minutes. App must supply the pull and keep sessions short.
- **Three pillars:** Fluency (pronunciation, where the reader stops, missed words; needs read-aloud) · Vocabulary (knowing and using words in the text; silent OK) · Comprehension (understanding; silent OK).
- **Progression:** (1) Baseline at onboarding gives a starting level per pillar; monthly re-assessment floated. (2) Every chapter feeds each pillar automatically. (3) One progress view: three tracks (starting level, current level, trend) + streak. (4) Level in each pillar sets difficulty of next text and next questions.
- **Evidence:** Fluency ← read-aloud pronunciation, pauses, missed words → shows fluency level, trend, words to practice. Vocabulary ← words looked up/tapped, word quiz in chapter-end check → shows vocabulary level, words learned this week. Comprehension ← chapter-end check score → shows comprehension level, score per chapter.
- **Silent reading:** fluency track pauses and says how to unlock it (read aloud); other two tracks keep updating.
- **Chapter-end check:** opens in the same screen, no menu/loading; three questions + one or two vocabulary words from the chapter; instant results update comprehension + vocabulary tracks; missed questions link back to the answering passage; next chapter unlocks on completion; pass mark open. Works silently (bus).
- **Engagement:** streaks (login + reading), Wordle-style daily puzzle (short text, three questions), levels/badges/points ("level 700", "ranked 37"), friends + competition, 5–10 minute sessions, up-front statistics (need real studies first).
- **Scope:** First version = baseline assessment, chapter reader, chapter-end check, vocabulary words, three-track progress view, streak, daily puzzle. Next = read-aloud fluency tracking, monthly re-assessment, friends and leaderboards, statistics-led marketing.
- **Open questions:** fluency in v1? check as gate or score? main user (children, teens, adult English learners)? content source (licensed, original, both)? level definition per pillar and scale?
