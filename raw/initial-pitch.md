# Team's initial pitch (verbatim intent, 2026-10-17)

Dynamic reading app. Users read stories out loud to the app. In real time, word by word, the AI grades reading ability. The app dynamically changes vocabulary complexity and keeps adjusting as the user reads more stories.

- Struggling reader: the same story is rewritten with simpler vocabulary at their level. Same story, different vocabulary complexity.
- Strong reader who loves a story: vocabulary made more complex so it's enjoyable to reread.
- Helps students adopt a more articulate vocabulary; makes reading more fun for people who struggle.
- **Keyword list**: users list words they want the AI to integrate into the stories they read, to practice adding them to their vocabulary.
- Uses human voice + AI listening to build a profile of the user and help them navigate the text.
- **Main goal: improve reading comprehension progressively.**
- Must integrate OCI ($500 credits). Voice features are core.
- Example used: Harry Potter.
