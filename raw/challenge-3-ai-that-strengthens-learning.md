# Challenge #3: AI That Strengthens Learning (Oracle) — as pasted by team, 2026-10-17

**How might we** responsibly use AI to improve learning, teaching, and educational access at every stage of life — while ensuring AI strengthens critical thinking, creativity, and human connection rather than becoming a substitute for them?

## Why this matters
AI is rapidly changing how learners study, how educators teach, and how institutions deliver education. It can expand access to practice, feedback, accessibility support, and instructional capacity. Education communities must address: how can AI support and deepen learning rather than simply complete work for learners?

## OCI / Oracle opportunity
Use OCI Generative AI and privacy technologies to create thoughtful experiences and to responsibly synthesize feedback or learning signals. May provide learners with a private, trusted channel to ask questions or share concerns; may provide educators with privacy-conscious, aggregated insight into the challenges affecting their student population.

## Who could benefit
- Learners across K-12, higher ed, workforce development, lifelong learning
- Educators, TAs, tutors, instructional designers
- Schools, universities, training providers, employers
- Families and communities seeking equitable access to quality learning

## Responsible innovation
Use synthetic, public, or appropriately de-identified data. Make clear when AI is assisting, what informs its outputs, and where learners and educators retain decision-making authority. Consider accessibility, transparency, bias, and appropriate human oversight.

## Challenge
Build a solution that uses AI to make learning more effective, accessible, engaging, or scalable while preserving learner agency and meaningful human oversight.

## What success looks like
- A compelling education problem with clear learner or educator value
- Evidence that the solution supports understanding and skill development, not just task completion
- A usable prototype with a credible path to adoption and scale
